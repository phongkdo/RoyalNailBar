Questions

Good to know before you visit

When does Royal Nail Bar open?

We are opening soon in The Shoppes at East Shore alongside the new Publix. Our opening date will be announced here and on our Google profile.

Where is Royal Nail Bar in Apopka?

We are opening in The Shoppes at East Shore, the Publix plaza at 2411 Ocoee Apopka Rd, Apopka, FL 32703, at the northeast corner of Ocoee-Apopka Rd and Keene Rd.

Do you offer microblading in Apopka?

Yes. Royal Nail Bar will offer eyebrow microblading and lip blushing by appointment, starting with a consultation to match shape and color to your face. Touch-up sessions are included in the first visit plan.

Do you have a nail membership or rewards program?

Yes. Crown Club members get a monthly manicure or pedicure plus discounts on other services, and every client earns Royal Rewards points: 1 point per dollar, with 100 points worth $5 off. Founding Members who join before opening day lock in their price for 12 months.

Can you accommodate group appointments and parties?

Yes. We welcome groups for bridal parties, birthdays, girls' nights, baby showers and team outings. Tell us your group size, preferred date and services in the booking form, and we'll reserve chairs side by side so everyone is pampered together.

How do I get Royal Nail Bar discounts and promotions?

Join the Royal List email list for 30% off your first visit, plus opening-day news and monthly promotions. We also offer 10% off during your birthday month, and 10% off for veterans and first responders with a valid ID.

Do you offer a military or first responder discount?

Yes. Veterans and first responders, including police, firefighters and EMTs, get 10% off every visit. Just show a valid military or department ID at checkout.

Will you take walk-ins?

Yes. Once we open, walk-ins will be welcome whenever a technician is free, and appointments will guarantee your time.

Is there parking?

Yes. The plaza has free parking shared with Publix, steps from our door.

How far are you from AdventHealth Apopka?

About half a mile, a two-minute drive. Lunch-break manicures for hospital staff will be easy to fit in.
